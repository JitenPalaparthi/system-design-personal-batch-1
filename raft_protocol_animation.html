What is happening?

Cluster initialized
All servers start as Followers. Each follower waits for a heartbeat from a leader. If no heartbeat arrives before its randomized election timeout, it becomes a Candidate.
Quorum / Majority
For N servers: floor(N / 2) + 1. With 5 nodes, a candidate needs 3 votes, including its own vote.

Event Log

Raft Roles

Follower: Passive state. Receives heartbeats/log entries and resets its election timer.

Candidate: Created when a follower times out. Increments the term, votes for itself, and asks peers for votes.

Leader: A candidate becomes leader after receiving a majority. It manages replication and sends heartbeats so followers do not start new elections.

Key Concepts

Term: A logical election epoch. Every new election increases the term.

Heartbeat: An empty/regular AppendEntries RPC sent by the leader. It proves the leader is alive.

Quorum: A strict majority. It prevents two isolated minorities from independently committing conflicting decisions.

Election timeout: Randomized per follower to reduce simultaneous candidacies and split votes.
